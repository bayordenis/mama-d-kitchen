Our story

From Mama's pot to your plate

Mama D Kitchen started with one coal pot and a simple promise: cook the food we grew up on, the same way it was cooked at home. No shortcuts on the stew, no rushing the fufu, and always enough shito to go round.

Today we cook dishes from every region of Ghana, from Fante kenkey on the coast to tuo zaafi from the North, using fresh ingredients bought from the market each morning.

  • Fresh dailyStews and soups made every morning, never reheated from yesterday.
  • Real recipesFamily recipes passed down, cooked over charcoal where it matters.
  • Your pepper, your wayMild, medium or "I am a true Ghanaian" hot.
